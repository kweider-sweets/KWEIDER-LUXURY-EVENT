DAMASCUS · OUR BEGINNINGS

Our beginnings,
in Damascus.

Our story began in Damascus, one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities, where sweet-making has long been woven into the city’s culture and hospitality.

In Al-Midan, Deeb bin Muhammad Kweider (1860–1951) laid the foundations of the family craft. Passed through generations, the Kweider name was formally established in 1969 — carrying authentic Damascene tradition forward.

FROM DAMASCUS TO LONDON

From Damascus
to London.

Since 1969, Kweider has carried the craft and hospitality of Damascus through generations.

Today, that heritage continues at our home on Acton High Street in West London — a destination for handcrafted baklava, traditional Syrian sweets, elegant gifts and a welcoming café.

منذ عام 1969، يحمل قويدر حرفة وضيافة دمشق عبر الأجيال، واليوم تستمر هذه المسيرة في مقرنا في Acton High Street بلندن.
